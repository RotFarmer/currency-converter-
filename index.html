Rates: ExchangeRate-API, updated daily, mid-market (banks and card providers may add fees). Text is read on your phone with Tesseract.js and photos are never uploaded. Location: your phone’s time zone, or GPS if you allow it (the country is looked up by BigDataCloud from a position rounded to about 1 km). Translation: on-device where the browser supports it, otherwise MyMemory (free, about 5,000 characters a day). Version .
